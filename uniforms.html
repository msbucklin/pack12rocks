Uniform

Uniforms have been an important part of Scouting since its beginnings — for both Scouts and adult volunteers. While their design has evolved over the decades, their purpose has remained the same: to represent equality, to communicate individual accomplishments, and to remind the wearer and others of his or her commitment to the ideals of Scouting. 

What is a Class A uniform and a Class B uniform?

Cub Scouts refer to two styles of dress for events and activities - "Class A" and "Class B"

"Class A” is the official scouting uniform and is worn to all Pack meetings, Den meetings, and Pack activities.

  • Adventure belt loops and rank badges are worn as part of the Class A uniform

  • Scouts may also purchase a red “brag” vest for their participation patches and overflow awards

“Class B” uniform is the Pack 12 t-shirt. Class B uniforms are typically worn to events where the activities could harm the Class A uniform. Scouts are also encouraged to wear their Class B uniform to school on Pack meeting days.

  • Every scout receives a Pack 12 "Class B" t-shirt when first joining the pack.
  • Additional shirts for scouts, parents, or siblings are available for purchase at most Pack meetings. 

 The Pack calendar will designate whether an event is "Class A" or "Class B."

 

Class A Dress Standards

Please see the official BSA Uniform guide for an example Class A uniform for each rank & patch placement. 

Lion - Kindergarten

The Lion Cub Scout uniform has the following parts:

  1. Shirt: The official Lion t-shirt.

  2. Belt: Official navy-blue web belt with metal buckle.
  3. Cap: Official navy-blue Lion cap.
  4. Neckerchief: Optional Lion neckerchief. The Lion den decides if they will wear the neckerchief.
  5. Neckerchief Slide: Optional Lion neckerchief slide.
  6. Patches: None

Official uniform pants, shorts, or skorts, and Cub Scout socks are optional.

Tiger - 1st Grade

The Tiger Cub Scout uniform has the following parts;

  1. Shirt: The official blue uniform shirt is available with long or short sleeves and has button-flap pockets.
  2. Belt: Official navy-blue web belt with metal buckle.
  3. Hat: Official blue hat with orange front and Tiger emblem.
  4. Neckerchief: Orange triangular neckerchief with Tiger logo. Official BSA neckerchiefs are the only neckerchiefs that are part of the uniform.
  5. Neckerchief slide: Official gold-tone metal slide with orange Tiger emblem. Cub Scouts may wear handmade neckerchief slides.
  6. Patches: US Flag, Council, World Crest Emblem, Unit Numberals (red "12"), Den Emblem (blue number)

Official uniform pants, shorts, or skorts, and Cub Scout socks are optional.

  4. Neckerchief: Red triangular neckerchief with Wolf logo. Official BSA neckerchiefs are the only neckerchiefs that are part of the uniform.

Webelos - 4th Grade

The Webelos Cub Scout uniform has the following parts:

Arrow of Light - 5th Grade

The Arrow of Light Cub Scout uniform has the following parts:

  1. Shirt: The official tan uniform shirt is available with long or short sleeves and has button-flap pockets.
  2. Belt: Official green web belt with BSA buckle.
  3. Hat: Optional – Arrow of Light Scouts may wear any official BSA hat.
  4. Neckerchief: Optional – Arrow of Light Scouts may wear any official BSA neckerchief.
  5. Neckerchief Slide: Official Scouts BSA slide with BSA emblem.
  6. Adventures Colors: The Adventure Colors are worn on the right sleeve to display the Arrow of Light Adventure Pins that have been earned.

  8. Patches: US Flag, Council, World Crest Emblem, Unit Numberals (red "12"), Den Emblem (blue number)

Official uniform pants, shorts, or skorts, and Cub Scout socks are optional.

 

Where to Buy

The Capitol Area Scout Shop is located in north Austin on the southbound frontage road just south of I-35 and Parmer Lane.

The staff is friendly and helpful and will help you find everything needed for your uniform. Please see the Capitol Area Scout Shop for hours and directions.

To purchase online, please follow the instructions at Capitol Area Scout Shop. Uniforms (except for the Council Patch) can also be purchased at scoutstuff.org, however shipping costs will apply. You can create a Wishlist on scoutstuff.org and send this to the Capitol Area Council Scout Shop for lower shipping costs or local pickup.

Where to Sew on Patches

Please refer to BSA Patch Placement for Cub Scouts for patch placement.

How to Sew on Patches

Patches may be either sewed or glued on. To glue on patches, use Badge Magic (available at scoutstuff.org or the Capitol Area Council Shop). For more information and how-to videos on how to glue on patches, please see Badge Magic for Boy Scouts.

The Capitol Area Council Scout Shop can sew on your required patches for an additional charge. Local seamstresses or dry cleaning/tailor shops can also sew on patches. 

To sew on patches by hand, match your thread color to the border color of the patch or use clear monofilament thread. Use a regular sewing needle and whip stitch around the border.

To sew on patches with a sewing machine, use a 100/16 denim/jean needle and use clear monofilament thread in the needle and thread to match the shirt color in the bobbin. Set your machine for a 2 1/2 - 3 straight stitch. Sew as close as possible to the interior edge of the border, moving slowly and advancing the needle by hand as necessary.
